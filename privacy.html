Privacy Policy

SleepSound — Sleep Sounds & White Noise

Effective date: October 29, 2026

English · 中文
In short: SleepSound works fully offline and does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics.

1. Data we collect

None. The app:

  • Does not require or offer any account or sign-in.
  • Does not ask for your name, email, location, or any personal information.
  • Has no internet permission and runs fully offline.

2. Permissions and why we use them

The app requests only the permissions needed to play audio. None of them access or transmit personal data:

  • Foreground service / media playback — to keep sound playing while your screen is off or the app is in the background.
  • Wake lock — to keep the device awake during playback, until the sleep timer ends.
  • Notifications — to show media controls in the notification bar and on the lock screen.

3. Local storage

All of your settings — language, scene configurations, sound mixes, volumes and favorites — are stored only on your device. We never have access to them, and they are never uploaded or synced anywhere.

4. Third-party services

The app uses no third-party SDKs, no advertising network, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. No data is shared with any third party.

5. Children's privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and the effective date above will be updated.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at smwuhua@gmail.com.
